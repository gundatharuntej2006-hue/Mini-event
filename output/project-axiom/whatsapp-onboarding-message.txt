👩‍🏫 *If your dashboard says “Mentor candidate”*
1. Choose *Mentor*, *Participant* or *Opt out* and confirm your decision.
2. If you choose Mentor, submit exactly one track: either Research, or Product. A Product may be software or hardware.
3. Keep the problem statement within 44 words and the explanation within 88 words.
4. After teams are formed, guide the members through the reviews and final showcase.

🧑‍💻 *If your dashboard says “Participant”*
1. Wait until all mentor problem statements are released together.
2. Accept the originality and confidentiality agreement.
3. Read the available projects, rank up to three preferences, and explain your proposed solution and contribution in your own words.
4. The more complete your preferences are, the better the team-matching process can work.

🗺️ *The complete journey*
Role confirmation → Problem-statement release → Participant preferences → Team formation → Review 1 on 29 November → Online Review 2 after the semester ends → Final showcase and winning project in the first week of next semester.
